Install

Up and running in a minute.

AdFree Tube isn't on the Play Store, so you install it directly — it takes three taps.

Download the APK

Tap Download for Android. Your browser may ask if you want to keep the file — choose Download anyway.

Allow the install

Open the file. If Android asks, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

Open and enjoy

Tap Install, then Open. The app tells you when a new version is out — just download again to update.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads. Videos stream straight from the source to your phone, so there are no servers to pay for.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. Subscriptions, history and Watch later are saved privately on your phone. You can import your existing subscriptions from Google Takeout in a few taps.

Is it safe to install?

The app is signed, has no ads or trackers, and asks only for the permissions it needs (notifications for upload alerts and the microphone for voice search, both optional). Android warns about any app installed outside the Play Store — that's expected.

How do I update?

When a new version is released, the app shows an "update available" banner. Tap it (or come back to this page) and install the new APK over the old one — your data stays.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes. Go to Library → Backup and save everything (subscriptions, playlists, history, watch progress) to a file. On the new phone, install AdFree Tube and restore from that file.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — AdFree Tube is Android-only for now.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Ad-free players like this aren't allowed on the Play Store, so it's distributed directly from this page, like other open video apps.
